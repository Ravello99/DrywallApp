What we build

Our crews handle the whole interior assembly, from the first chalk line to the last ceiling tile, so one contractor carries the wall from layout to finish.

Steel stud framing

Layout, track and studs, bulkheads and soffits, backing and blocking, and shaft wall.

Drywall boarding

Walls, ceilings and bulkheads in regular, fire-rated, moisture-resistant and abuse-resistant board.

Taping and finishing

Tape, coats and sanding to the finish level the spec calls for, up to Level 5 where light is critical.

Insulation and sound

Batt insulation, acoustic batts in demising walls, vapour barrier and sealing.

Acoustic ceilings

T-bar grid and ceiling tile, with clean cut-ins around lights, diffusers and sprinkler heads.

Secure and rated assemblies

Secure-Lok Plus security ceilings and STC-rated wall assemblies for institutional work.

Projects we take on

We bid three kinds of work, and we staff and schedule each one differently.

Multi-family residential

Apartment and condo buildings, townhouses and mixed-use residential.

  • Repetitive unit layouts framed and boarded floor by floor
  • Demising walls built to their sound rating
  • Crews sized to keep pace with the trades behind us

Commercial fit-outs

Restaurants, storefronts, offices and other tenant improvements.

  • Tight schedules and night or weekend work in occupied malls
  • Feature bulkheads and ceilings that meet brand standards
  • Clean, quick turnover for the next trade

Institutional

Hospitals, schools, universities and data centres.

  • Fire-rated and STC-rated assemblies built to spec
  • Secure-Lok Plus and abuse-resistant systems
  • Infection-control and site-security requirements followed

How we work

Accurate pricing up front means fewer extras later, and keeps the job on time and on budget.

  1. Estimate

    We take off the drawings carefully, so the price you get is the price you pay unless the scope changes.

  2. Plan

    Every scope gets an hour budget and a crew that matches it: framers, boarders, tapers and ceiling installers.

  3. Build

    Our foremen track progress daily, raise RFIs early and request material before it holds up the work.

  4. Close out

    Deficiencies done, site cleaned, and progress draws that line up with the work in place.

What general contractors get from us

We run every job on our own project system, so you get straight answers about manpower, progress and paperwork.

Talk to us about your project

  • Real manpower counts

    Crew clock in by GPS on your site, so the number of people we say are there is the number that are there.
